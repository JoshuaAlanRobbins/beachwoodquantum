Section 02 • Physical & Research Track

Physical Field Model: Anisotropic Transport & Loss

For lattice and transport research—such as anisotropic Kagome structures like CsV3Sb5 and directional energy routing—the field model is framed around measurable directional flux, anisotropic conductivity, and real spatial damping.

Physical Transport & Damping Balance EQ-P1
Jphys(r, t) = σeff(r) · Edrive(r, t) − γloss(r) · Jphys(r, t)
Dimensional & Explicit Steady-State Form: Because Jphys appears on both sides without an explicit time derivative, γloss(r) acts as a dimensionless spatial damping / impedance tensor. Solving algebraically yields the directly testable steady-state response: Jphys(r, t) = (I + γloss(r))−1 σeff(r) · Edrive(r, t) When transient relaxation timescales are measured directly in seconds (s−1 scattering rates), the damping term pairs with an explicit relaxation operator τrel ∂Jphys/∂t to preserve dimensional homogeneity.
Symbol Quantity Physical Interpretation
Jphys(r, t) Local Physical Flux Density Charge, heat, or energy flow density measured at spatial coordinate r and time t.
σeff(r) Effective Anisotropic Transport Tensor Captures preferred low-resistance pathways versus high-resistance or blocked directions across the lattice or physical geometry.
Edrive(r, t) Applied Driving Gradient External or boundary-applied driving potential, thermal gradient, or field excitation.
γloss(r) Spatial Damping / Scattering Tensor Dimensionless dissipation factor (or rate-normalized impedance) capturing real physical loss that must be measured rather than assumed away.

Model Assumptions

Explicit Validity Regime

  • Anisotropic Geometry: Preferred transport axes exist in the underlying structure (e.g., z = 4 Kagome networks such as CsV3Sb5 or engineered routing lattices).
  • Bounded Linear Drive: Effective conductivity tensor σeff(r) holds within bounded excitation amplitudes before nonlinear saturation.
  • Non-Zero Dissipation: Spatial loss γloss(r) is strictly non-negative and empirically bounded across temperature and load regimes.

Validation & Falsification

Measurements Required to Test or Disprove

  • Directional Flux Mapping: Measure orthogonal flux ratios under controlled drive sweeps to verify whether off-axis suppression exceeds sensor noise.
  • Empirical Damping Extraction: Quantify thermal and resistive losses directly to fit (I + γloss)−1 rather than treating loss as a free residual.
  • Falsification Condition: Model requires nonlinear or time-derivative revision if measured flux diverges from linear proportionality across the operating drive band.

Section 03 • Systems & Control Track

Systems Field Model: Delay-Bounded State & Dispatch

For runtime control, telemetry, and community microgrid orchestration—C++20 zero-allocation control loops, high-rate sensor ingest, and edge dispatch—the systems layer tracks discrete state evolution with explicit pipeline latency and bounded noise.

Discrete-Time State Evolution & Delayed Observation EQ-S1 / EQ-S2
xk+1 = Adxk + Bduk + wk, yk = Cdxk−τk + vk
Stability Requirement: Control feedback uk = π(yk, τk) must remain bounded across the worst-case measured pipeline delay 0 ≤ τk ≤ τmax. Exceeding τmax transitions the node automatically into a pre-defined deterministic fault or local islanding state.
Symbol Component Operational Role
xk System State Vector Physical state at discrete step k (bus voltage/frequency, storage state-of-charge, thermal or mechanical state).
uk Control Action Vector Deterministic dispatch, load shedding, breaker switching, or field actuation command issued at step k.
Ad, Bd, Cd Discretized Hardware Operators State transition, input coupling, and observation matrices calibrated directly against physical hardware response.
τk Telemetry & Pipeline Delay Explicitly timestamped ingest and compute lag at step k so control stability accounts for real measurement delay.
wk, vk Process & Sensor Noise Unmodeled field disturbances (wk) and measurement noise (vk) bounded via empirical calibration.

Telemetry Constraints

Pipeline & Runtime Discipline

  • Explicit Lag Accounting: Every telemetry packet carries a monotonic hardware capture timestamp so τk is measured per loop tick, never ignored.
  • Deterministic Loop Budget: Zero-allocation C++20 runtime paths prevent garbage-collection jitter or unbounded latency spikes during switching events.
  • Noise Floor Calibration: Sensor covariance bounds on vk are profiled per transducer before enabling closed-loop actuation.

Mandatory Runtime Invariants

Conservation, Bounds & Fault States

  • Conservation Invariant: Metered energy balance across generation, storage delta, load draw, and loss must close within tolerance every cycle.
  • State & Actuator Bounds: Hard safety clamps enforce bus voltage, frequency, thermal ceiling, and storage SOC limits independent of high-level optimization.
  • Deterministic Fault States: Packet loss, sensor disagreement, or τk > τmax triggers explicit load-shedding priority and safe islanding.

Section 04 • Empirical Record

Open Calibration Log

A running engineering record of where the equations meet real hardware, laboratory measurements, or field data—and explicitly where assumptions must be tightened or corrected. No simulated results are logged as field truth.

Standard 01 • Falsifiability

Anisotropy Anchored in Operators

Writing σeff(r) and γloss(r) turns prose about directional preference or reduced friction into a clear structural distinction between transport, driving gradient, and physical loss.

Standard 03 • Living Pre-Print
