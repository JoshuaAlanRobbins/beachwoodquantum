Transparent Economics & Knowledge Transfer

Turning 200 Years of Misunderstood “Liabilities” Into Legible Well Files

Headline estimates often paint aging Lower 48 wells as a 200-year liability backlog. In practice, the deeper crisis is human: veteran operational craft is leaving the field without being transferred, leaving the next generation unsure how to optimize—or even turn on—producing assets. GEM bridges that gap at the individual API level.

01 • Living API Well Files

Costs, Plans, Logs & Optimization per Well

Each mapped API well develops a living file containing tangible job costs, surface designs, historical production points, logs, graphs, and field optimization plans—giving banking and operations one shared reference record.

02 • Transparent Economics

Probability of Success per Dollar Spent

Using first-party equipment quotes and supply sources, future expenses are known up front. Lower-cost, high-probability jobs validate and fund larger, more capital-intensive projects based on tangible profitability.

03 • Operational Brilliance

Preserving Hands-On Field Craft

Knowing the exact heater-treater, concrete pad scope, line pipe joints, check valves, and 700 lb ball valves needed to make oil readily saleable is operational brilliance—now structured so a new generation can execute cleanly.

Future Processing & Modular Site Concepts

As physical equipment and shipping logistics are validated on live field locations, GEM explores downsizing traditional midstream functions into compact, instrumented field sites where hardware and digital telemetry work hand in hand:

  • Modular Field Pads & Energy Libraries Compact on-location sites combining housed field processing equipment, live data terminals, and Energy Libraries so operators and next-generation teams learn directly beside active iron.
  • Certified Direct-to-Downstream Mineral & Elemental Streams Exploratory scientific analysis of commercial co-products from produced streams—including sulfur isotopes (S-32, S-33, S-34, S-36), cinnabar / HgS, sodium, and chloride—subject to strict labeling, certification, and physical field validation.
  • In-Line Produced Water, CO₂ & Facility Efficiency Investigating in-line saltwater hydrogeneration paired with power purchase agreements, closed-loop CO₂ reinjection for carbon energy credits, facility energy-savings performance contracts, and temperature-specific subsurface laboratories.
