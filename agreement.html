THIS FEE AGREEMENT (this “Agreement”) is made and entered into as of   (the “Effective Date”) by and between   (“Client”) and The Beachwood Helix Corporation (“Beachwood” or “Provider”). The parties hereto, in consideration of their mutual agreements and covenants hereinafter set forth and intending to be legally bound hereby, covenant and agree as follows:

1. Services Rendered by Provider. During the Term (as defined in Section 5), Provider shall use its best efforts to assist Client in contacting and eliciting interest from an Interested Party or Interested Parties (as defined in Section 2) to close a Transaction (as defined in Section 4) with Client (the “Services”). The Services that Provider shall provide under this Agreement shall be in addition to, and independent of, any other services which Provider may be expected to provide to Client under any other agreement(s) that Provider may have with Client. Accordingly, no portion of the compensation which Provider may earn under such other agreement(s) will have any relationship to the Provider’s Fee (as defined in Section 3(b)), nor will payment of any Provider’s Fee have any relationship to any other sums for which Client may be obligated to Provider.
2. Interested Party.
  • (a) “Interested Party” shall mean an entity introduced to an authorized representative of Client by Provider (i) in accordance with Section 2(c) hereof, (ii) solely for the purpose of Closing (as defined in Section 3(e)) a Transaction, and (iii) that is not subject to a Prior Relationship (as defined in Section 2(b)).
  • (b) “Prior Relationship” shall mean any entity, opportunity, sale, transaction, funding or other agreement with which Client or any of its representatives has a prior business relationship or of which Client or any of its representatives is aware prior to or as of the date Provider introduces such party to Client.
3. Fees and Payments; Closing.
  • (a) In consideration of the Services, Client agrees to pay Provider on the following schedule:
    • (i) $9,000.00 for the initial 90 (ninety) day commitment.
    • (ii) Following the initial 90 (ninety) day commitment, Client agrees to pay $3,000.00, billed on a recurring 30 (thirty) day billing cycle.
    • (iii) After the initial 90 (ninety) day period, either party may elect to discontinue the engagement at any time and pay any outstanding invoice within 45 days of written notice to the other party.
  • (b) Client shall pay a cash fee to Provider (the “Provider’s Fee”) equal to (i) three percent (3.0%) of Consideration (as defined in Section 3(d)).
  • (c) Payment of the Provider’s Fee by Client shall not occur prior to the Closing of the Transaction and shall otherwise occur upon or within three (3) days after the Closing of the Transaction; provided, that, if any portion of the Consideration is not payable at the Closing (any such deferred Consideration, the “Deferred Consideration”), Client shall not be liable, and shall not pay, to Provider any portion of the Provider’s Fee derived from such Deferred Consideration until such time as the Deferred Consideration is actually paid to the Interested Party. No other fee (including any expense or cost incurred by Provider with respect to the Interested Party or pursuant to this Agreement) other than the Provider’s Fee shall be payable to Provider by Client without express written approval of each such fee by Client. The Provider’s Fee and payment thereof is expressly contingent upon the Closing of the Transaction.
  • (d) “Consideration” shall mean the cash invested solely by Client in the Interested Party or the assets of the Interested Party at Closing of the Transaction.
  • (e) “Closing” shall mean a cash investment by Client in an Interested Party or the assets of an Interested Party as evidenced by the negotiation, execution and delivery of definitive documentation and wire transfers mutually acceptable to Client (in its sole discretion) and an Interested Party and which takes place during the Term.
4. Transaction.
  • (a) “Transaction” shall mean the negotiation and Closing of a transaction or series of transactions that involve funding or the purchase of a company or companies or assets of companies, including, among others, oil and gas assets and properties; provided, that a Transaction shall not (i) mean any of the potential opportunities, sales, transactions, fundings or other agreements which are currently known to Client or its representatives including, without limitation, Prior Relationships.
  • (b) Client shall not be required to enter into any Transaction except with parties and on terms and conditions that are satisfactory to Client in its sole discretion and are accepted by Client in its sole discretion. Nothing in this Agreement or otherwise should be construed as a commitment or obligation by Client to proceed with any Transaction or to engage in any further discussions regarding any Transaction with anyone. Even if discussions concerning any Transaction do take place, Client shall be free to conduct the process for evaluating any Transaction as Client, in its sole discretion, shall determine (including changing or terminating any such process), and Client shall be free, in its sole discretion, to at any time accept or reject any proposal from an Interested Party or anyone relating to any Transaction, just as any Interested Party shall be free, in its sole discretion, to make or not make any offer.
5. Term; Exclusivity.
  • (a) Term is the duration agreed to in Section 3(a) and as agreed to in Section 5(b).
  • (b) Following the termination of this Agreement, (a) all rights and obligations of the parties hereto shall terminate except for those rights and obligations contained in Sections 7, 8 and 9 of this Agreement, which shall survive termination of this Agreement indefinitely and (b) Provider shall only be entitled to the Provider’s Fee, as calculated in, and pursuant to, Section 3 hereof with regard to any Transaction for which there occurred a Closing during the Term or the twelve (12) month period immediately following the Term.
6. Representations.
  • (a) Client and Provider each acknowledge that no representations, inducements, promises or agreements, oral or written, have been made by Client or Provider, or anyone acting on behalf of Client or Provider, which are not contained in this Agreement and none of the parties hereto shall be bound by or liable for any alleged representation, promise, inducement or statement of intention not so set forth herein.
  • (b) Provider represents and warrants that it:
    • (i) has full and complete authority to enter into this Agreement;
    • (ii) is an independent contractor and is not and shall not be considered Client’s agent for any purposes whatsoever;
    • (iii) shall not represent itself as an agent of Client; and
    • (iv) is not granted any right or authority to assume or create any obligations or liability, express or implied, on Client’s behalf, or to negotiate on behalf of or bind Client in any manner whatsoever.
7. Confidentiality / Insider Information. Provider may not disclose this Agreement, the contents thereof, information processes, know-how, or any other nonpublic information related to Client or its investment activity, to any person without the prior written consent of Client (in its sole discretion). Provider agrees not to trade in the public securities of Client without the prior written consent of Client (in its sole discretion).
8. Indemnification. Provider agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Client and its officers, directors, employees, affiliates, advisors, agents and controlling persons and entities (each, an “Indemnified Person”) from and against any and all losses, claims, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including, without limitation, all attorneys’ fees and expenses of settlement, litigation or preparation therefor) which any Indemnified Person may incur or which may be asserted against any Indemnified Person by any other person arising out of or in connection with any investigation, litigation or proceeding (the “Indemnified Liabilities”) related to or arising from this Agreement and any person or entity seeking fees or compensation in any form for services rendered with respect to a Transaction, where such person acted in concert with Provider.
9. Miscellaneous.
  • (a) This Agreement contains the entire agreement of the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior negotiations, agreements and understandings with respect thereto, both written and oral. This Agreement may not be contradicted by evidence of prior, contemporaneous or subsequent oral agreements of the parties. There are no unwritten or oral agreements between the parties.
  • (b) This Agreement does not give rise now or in the future to an agency or partnership relationship between Provider and Client or any of its affiliates, nor to any right of Provider to inspect or audit any records or documents of Client or any of its affiliates.
  • (c) Any amendment, change or modification of this Agreement shall be void unless by a formal written instrument signed by Client and by Provider. All provisions of this Agreement are severable, and the unenforceability or invalidity of any of the provisions of this Agreement shall not affect the validity or enforceability of the remaining provisions of this Agreement. Should any part of this Agreement be held unenforceable, the unenforceable portion or portions shall be removed (and no more), and the remaining portions of this Agreement shall be enforced as fully as possible (removing the minimum amount possible).
  • (d) The terms and conditions of this Agreement shall inure to the benefit of, and be binding upon, the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.
